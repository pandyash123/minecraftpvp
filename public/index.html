A browser Minecraft-style kit PvP arena. Fight bots, or invite friends.

Only applies when you're the only player - otherwise use /arena in chat.

Enchantments (armor/sword/axe/bow) are in their own panel below.

Controls — WASD move · Space jump · Shift sneak · Ctrl/R sprint · Left click attack / mine (hold with the spear to charge a stronger thrust) · Right click place / shoot / throw a potion (splashes on impact, hold for bow & apple) · hold Right click with sword/pickaxe/axe to raise your shield (blocks what's in front of you, but not hits from well above or below) · the axe hits harder, and its first hit through a raised shield still gets blocked but disables that shield for 3s · bots can wear a shield down too - repeated blocked hits in a row disable it (3 on Easy, 2 on Normal, 1 on Hard) · 1-9 / scroll wheel switch item · E inventory (drag items to/from your backpack) · F5 or the view button cycles 1st/3rd person · Enter chat · Esc pause / release mouse
