Blockcraft Arena

A browser Minecraft-style kit PvP arena. Fight bots, or invite friends.

Enchantments (armor/sword/axe/bow) are in their own panel below.

Controls — WASD move · Space jump · Shift sneak · Ctrl/R sprint · Left click attack / mine (hold with the spear to charge a stronger thrust) · Right click place / shoot / throw a potion (splashes on impact, hold for bow & apple) · hold Right click with sword/pickaxe/axe to raise your shield (blocks what's in front of you, but not hits from well above or below) · the axe hits harder, and its first hit through a raised shield still gets blocked but disables that shield for 3s · bots can wear a shield down too - repeated blocked hits in a row disable it (3 on Easy, 2 on Normal, 1 on Hard) · 1-9 / scroll wheel switch item · E inventory (drag items to/from your backpack) · Enter chat · Esc pause / release mouse

Commands — /bots <0-16> [difficulty] [armor] [kit] [weapon] · /difficulty <easy|normal|hard|random> · /botarmor <none|leather|iron|diamond> · /botkit <sword|axe|web> · /botweapon <fixed|versatile|full> · /botteam <on|off> · /kit <sword|axe|web> · /botdiff <bot name> <level> · /dummy <0-3> · /atkdummy <0-3>
